About the job
Data Analyst – Client Success | Finovate Global

Location: Gurugram (in-office), Bengaluru (in-office) | Experience: 0–3 years | Function: Analytics & Client Success

The opportunity:



India's ₹40+ lakh crore lending ecosystem runs on one thing after disbursal: collections. 

It is one of the largest, most manual, most broken workflows in financial services and Finovate Global is rebuilding it with agentic AI.



AVIO, our AI platform, autonomously conducts collections conversations for India's leading NBFCs and financial institutions. It has crossed 10 Lakh+ (1 million) calls per day with ZERO Humans involved and is scaling toward 3 million calls a day. Also, interacting via Whatsapp, Emails, SMS and holding billions of interactions in a month.



Every one of those interactions generates data: who answered, what was said, what was promised, what was paid. Buried in that data is the answer to the only question our clients care about, how do we recover more, faster, at lower cost?



We're hiring a Data Analyst – Client Success to own that question end to end.





What you'll own

Campaign design. Take client portfolio data and design the calling strategy: segmentation, timing, channel mix, retry logic, bot persona selection. You decide who gets called, when, and how.
Outcome analytics. Measure everything: connect rates, PTP rates, kept rates, recovery percentages, cost per rupee collected. Diagnose what's working, kill what isn't, and run the next iteration.
Client reporting. Build and present the reports our clients run their reviews on. You'll sit across from client teams, walk them through the numbers, defend the strategy, and convert their feedback into the next campaign.
Continuous improvement. This is not one-and-done reporting. You'll run a standing measure > analyse > improve loop on every live portfolio, and your campaigns' recovery numbers are your scorecard.
Why this role is different



Your analysis moves crores. A 1% lift in recovery rate on our portfolio is not a dashboard metric, it's real money our clients see in their books. Few analyst roles anywhere offer feedback loops this direct.
You own the client room. Most analysts hand their work to someone else to present. Here, the person who did the analysis is the person the client hears it from. You'll build genuine client-management skill years before your peers.
Production AI, not spreadsheet theatre. You'll work on top of a live agentic AI system, tuning how autonomous voice agents behave at scale through the campaigns you design.
Founder proximity. You work directly with the CEO. Good analysis becomes strategy in days.


Who we're looking for

0–3 years of experience. Exceptional freshers with demonstrated analytical rigour are strongly encouraged.
Deep, genuine love for data — you're fluent in Excel/Google Sheets at an advanced level; SQL and Python/dashboarding (Power BI, Metabase, or similar) are strong pluses.
Execution bias. You'd rather ship the campaign and learn from the numbers than perfect the plan.
Client-ready communication — you can turn a messy analysis into a crisp narrative and hold your ground in a review meeting.
Structured, first-principles thinking; comfort with ambiguity and ownership.
Exposure to lending, collections, or BFSI data is a plus, not a requirement — we'll teach you the domain.
